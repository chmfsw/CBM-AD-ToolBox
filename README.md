The CBM-AD Toolbox was used to identify robust metabolic biomarkers and therapeutic target combinations for Alzheimer’s disease. The folders are organized as follows:
A0-Model-CABL-v2: Contains six disease–healthy model pairs representing asymptomatic and symptomatic Alzheimer’s disease in the entorhinal cortex (EC), frontal cortex (FC), and temporal cortex (TC).
Metabolic-Alteration: Implements parsimonious multi-objective metabolite flow variability analysis to determine feasible ranges of metabolite flow rates under each nutrient condition and identify medium-independent metabolic biomarkers.
Target-Intervention: Solves a fuzzy multi-objective hierarchical optimization problem using a nested hybrid differential evolution algorithm to identify potential therapeutic enzyme targets and their combinations.
XML-Reconstructed-Models: Reconstructs the AsymAD and AD models and their corresponding healthy models, then saves them in XML format.
